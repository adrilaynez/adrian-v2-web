**titan-engine** holds my notebooks following Andrej Karpathy's *Zero to Hero*: autograd from scratch, character-level models and a small GPT trained on **Don Quixote**.

The name comes from the original plan, a GPT-2 inference engine in C++ and CUDA. That part is not built: the repo is Python only.
